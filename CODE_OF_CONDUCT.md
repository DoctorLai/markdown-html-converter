# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels, discuss ideas on their technical merits, and accept feedback without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers using a non-public contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct harms the community.

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
